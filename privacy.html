Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auremont Digital ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and mobile applications.

Information we collect

We aim to collect as little data as possible. Depending on the app, we may collect:

  • Basic technical information (device model, OS version, crash logs) to fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Information you choose to send us, such as your email address when you contact support.

How we use information

We use information only to operate, maintain and improve our apps, respond to support requests, and keep our services secure.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers (for example, analytics or crash reporting) who process it on our behalf, or when required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
